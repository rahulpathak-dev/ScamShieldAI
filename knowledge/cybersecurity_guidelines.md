# Cybersecurity Guidelines

- Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVV numbers, seed phrases, private keys, or recovery codes.
- Verify financial messages through official apps, official websites, or known phone numbers.
- Do not click shortened or unexpected links in banking, investment, delivery, tax, or crypto messages.
- Treat urgent payment demands, legal threats, and account suspension warnings as high-risk until independently verified.
- Do not move conversations to WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, or external email when discussing banking or investment accounts.
- Report suspicious financial messages to the relevant platform, bank, or cybercrime authority.
