# Fraud Examples

## Banking Phishing

A message says an account is suspended and asks the user to verify an OTP through a link. This combines urgency, fear, authority impersonation, and credential theft.

## Crypto Investment Scam

A message promises guaranteed high returns in crypto or forex trading and asks for an initial deposit. This uses greed, scarcity, and fake trust.

## Job Scam

A recruiter promises remote work and asks for a registration fee, training fee, or identity documents before a real interview.

## Romance Scam

A person builds trust or affection and then asks for emergency money, gift cards, or crypto transfers.
