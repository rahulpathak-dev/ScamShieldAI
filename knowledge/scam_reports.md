# Scam Reports Knowledge Base

## Report: Fake Bank Verification

Users reported SMS messages claiming to be from a bank security department. The message said accounts would be blocked unless OTP verification was completed immediately.

## Report: Delivery Fee Scam

Users reported delivery messages asking for a small customs or redelivery fee through a shortened link.

## Report: Crypto Doubling Scam

Users reported social media ads promising to double Bitcoin or USDT deposits within 24 hours.
