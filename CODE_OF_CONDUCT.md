# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in ScamShieldAI welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying accountable for impact.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Handle cybersecurity topics responsibly and legally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing live malicious infrastructure, credentials, or exploit instructions outside a safe research context.
- Abusing the project or community for fraud, spam, or harm.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
